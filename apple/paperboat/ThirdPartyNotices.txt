PaperPad Boat — Apple shell and native controls by the PaperPad contributors.
Built on Harbour Masters PaperBoat, its Paper Mario decompilation/DX work,
JeodC's libultraship and Torch integration, and their upstream contributors.
PaperBoat: https://github.com/HarbourMasters/PaperBoat (CC0 source notice).
libultraship: https://github.com/JeodC/libultraship (MIT; nested terms retained).
Torch: https://github.com/JeodC/Torch-LH (MIT; nested terms retained).

other linked components retain their own licenses. Exact notices are collected
in Licenses/ and selected source identities in BUILD_PROVENANCE.json.
Nintendo game material and trademarks remain their owners' property. Users
supply the supported game locally; this package contains no ROM or extracted
